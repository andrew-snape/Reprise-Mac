# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 0.76

- Music: Crossfade now listens to the songs it has downloaded, fading on a bar line, skipping silence at the end of one song and the start of the next.
- Now Playing, the media keys and headphone buttons use macOS's new Now Playing system, with proper details for episodes, movies, songs and radio stations.

## 0.75

- Music and radio: the AirPlay button no longer pulses while you're playing to a speaker; it turns blue, and dims when there's nothing to AirPlay to.
- Music: a new trial setting, AirPlay From Reprise Only (Settings > Music), sends just Reprise to the speaker instead of all your Mac's sound.

## 0.74

- Movies and TV: minimize the player into the bar at the bottom of the window and keep browsing while it plays. Click the picture to bring it back.

## 0.73

- No changes to the app; the project page now has screenshots.

## 0.72

- Rivulet is now Reprise, with a new icon. Your settings, sign-ins and updates carry over. After updating, the app file may still be called Rivulet.app; quit it and rename it in Finder if you like.

## 0.69

- Music: the waveform shows each song's quiet and loud parts instead of a solid block.

## 0.68

- Music: lyrics in Now Playing, following the song when Plex has them timed; click a line to jump to it.
- Music: Now Playing's seek bar shows the shape of the song.
- Music: Now Playing's background uses Plex's own colours from the cover.
- Music: a History tab lists every song you've played from the library, by day.
- Music: Sonic Path to Here, in a song's menu, fills Up Next with songs that drift from what's playing to that one.
- Music: Plex and its other apps see what's queued here.

## 0.67

- Music: tracks no longer stop for half a minute when the next one starts loading or downloading (0.66 fixed this only in part).
- Music: Download Ahead gives way to whatever is playing.

## 0.66

- Music: a large lossless track no longer starts, stops for half a minute, then carries on.

## 0.65

- Rows fit the window: tiles grow to fill the width and the last one is cut off on purpose, so it is clear there is more.
- Rows settle on a tile when you scroll, and arrows under the pointer move a row a screenful at a time.
- Music: Recently Played shows what you chose to play (the album, artist, playlist or song), newest first.

## 0.64

- Picture in Picture for Plex titles: the button beside Full Screen, or ⌃⌘P. Browse the app while the picture floats.
- On your home network, Rivulet now prefers your server's secure address over plain HTTP.
- Newer playback engine (AetherEngine 7.67.1).
- Music: your queue comes back after you quit, paused where you left it.
- Music: rate artists, albums and songs by clicking the stars, or from the right-click menu.
- Music: an artist's page groups releases into Albums, Singles & EPs, Live, Compilations and more, and lists Your Most Played.
- Music: play counts and when you last played something show on artist and album pages and in Now Playing. Sort a library by Last Played or Most Played.
- Music: optional crossfade between songs (Settings > Music). Album tracks that run together stay gapless.

## 0.63

- VoiceOver reads a name for every icon-only button.
- Download Ahead carries on after a download is cancelled, instead of stalling.
- M3U playlists that start with a byte order mark, or use Latin-1 text, now load.
- The log no longer includes stream addresses or sign-in tokens.
